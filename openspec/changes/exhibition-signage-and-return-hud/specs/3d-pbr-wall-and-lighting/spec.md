## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: In-world Brushed Aluminum exhibition title banner, painted home wayfinding link, and page title
The public gallery wall SHALL render an in-world 2 × 1 exhibition title banner (`ExhibitionTitleSign`) inside `.wall-world` in the upper-left margin, top- and bottom-aligned flush with the true top-left Row-0 album placard, and included in initial Row-0 framing via `includeTitleSign(bounds, placements)`:
- **Banner geometry:** `~452px` wide (`2 × 214px` album placard width **plus** the `24px` grid-column gutter, `TITLE_SIGN_WIDTH = 4.52u`) and `200px` tall (exact album placard height, `TITLE_SIGN_HEIGHT = 2.0u`). The horizontal gap to the adjacent album placard SHALL be the shared `columnGutter` measured from that album placard's right edge to the first framed photo's left edge (structural `PLACARD_GAP +` the frame's mat inset), so banner → placard and placard → photo read as the same visual gutter. The banner top and bottom edges SHALL be pixel-flush with that album placard. The banner's outer left edge (`titlePlacard.x`) is the master left datum, and the `← home` link SHALL be anchored directly to it so both move leftward in lockstep.
- **Overview camera fit:** The Row-0 fit SHALL span from `titlePlacard.x` to the rightmost framed photo's right edge, with the same `outerWallMargin` applied on both sides (`fitBoundsToViewport`), so the empty wall padding matches and the rightmost photo column is never cropped. `includeTitleSign` derives the banner's x from the top-left album placard's left edge minus `columnGutter` minus `TITLE_SIGN_WIDTH`.
- **Surface materiality:** Reuse the album placards' existing **Beveled Acrylic Glass** material verbatim by widening `.museum-placard.mat-acrylic` rules to `:is(.museum-placard, .exhibition-title-placard).mat-acrylic`: dark smoked translucent plate, bevel borders, `var(--glass-ink)` text, cylindrical bottom-left/bottom-right `.placard-standoff`s, soft cast shadow, PR #17 Alabaster plate, and the coarse-pointer static frosted fallback. No new material treatment.
- **Title treatment (`greg's photo wall`):** Bold Helvetica (`32px`, roughly `1.8×–2× --font-size-placard-title`, `font-weight: 700`, tight `1.05` line-height), stacked flush-left in the upper-left across two intentional lines (`greg's` / `photo wall`), leaving the right half and lower plaque open glass space.
- **Wayfinding Home Link (`← home`):** `14px` directly beneath the banner and flush-left with the banner's left edge (not centered), wrapped in `<a href="https://gregory-lazatin.com">` at the standard understated `13.5px` / `font-weight: 500` using mood-adaptive painted ink (`#111111` on `alabaster`, `#e5e3dc` on `concrete`, `#d6d4cd` on `chiaroscuro`). Under `@media (hover: hover) and (pointer: fine)`, hovering SHALL shift the `←` arrow `3px` left (`transform: translateX(-3px)`) with a subtle contrast shift and **no underline**. `GalleryCanvas`'s native `onPointerDown` and `onClick` handlers SHALL ignore targets inside `.exhibition-sign-column` so clicking or dragging starting on `← home` never initiates a wall pan or background overview click.
- **Browser Tab Title:** The root layout (`src/app/layout.tsx`) SHALL set `metadata.title` to `"greg's photo wall"`.

#### Scenario: Banner and home link render flush with the top-left album placard and navigate without dragging
- **WHEN** the gallery loads on Row 0 with at least one album, and a visitor clicks or drags starting on `← home`
- **THEN** the `greg's` / `photo wall` banner SHALL render in `.wall-world` as a `452 × 200px` Beveled Glass plaque whose top and bottom edges are flush with the adjacent album placard and whose gap to it equals the visible album placard → first framed-photo gutter, `← home` SHALL sit flush-left beneath it and link directly to `https://gregory-lazatin.com` with no underline on hover, and the native `pointerdown`/`pointermove` sequence on `.exhibition-sign-column` SHALL NOT pan the wall

#### Scenario: Browser tab displays greg's photo wall
- **WHEN** the root layout metadata is evaluated
- **THEN** `metadata.title` SHALL equal `"greg's photo wall"`
